## ⚡ Quick Start & Offline Activation

1. **Install PluginXpert Studio** on your device.
2. Launch the app — it opens in **Guest / Unlicensed Mode**.
3. Click **⚡ Activate License** in the top header or when opening a product.
4. Copy your **Hardware ID (HWID)** and send it to your PluginXpert Admin.
5. Enter the generated **License Key (PX1-...)** to permanently unlock downloads and guides.
